# Da Mario — the story of the Vegetariana

## Where it comes from

The market in Padua runs under the arcades of the Palazzo della Ragione, and the sellers price courgettes at each other across the square all morning. Elena worked a stall there for two summers as a student and learned which vegetables are worth cooking and which are only worth looking at.

Elena worked the Padua stall in the summers of 2006 and 2007, for a woman called Nadia who sold nothing she had not grown and shouted the prices of her courgettes across the square until the arcade echoed. The phrase *the sad pizza* is Nadia's, about something she was served in Venice, and it has been in this kitchen's vocabulary ever since.

## What the creator had in mind

She built this one against what she calls *the sad pizza*: a Margherita with three cold vegetables dropped on top. The rule is that every vegetable is cooked in the way that suits it before it ever sees the dough — four pans, four methods, and a pizza that takes the kitchen longer than anything else on the menu.

## Why these ingredients

Courgette, pepper, mushroom and onion are the four the market always has. They are on the pizza because they are always available, which is an honest reason and the real reason.

All four are ingredients that punish laziness in the same way: each of them is more than eighty per cent water, and each of them will give that water back to a pizza at the worst possible moment. Cooking them separately is not a refinement, it is the minimum that makes the dish possible, which is why the recipe has four pans and no shortcuts.

## How we prepare it

Courgettes are sliced and grilled dry. Peppers are roasted and peeled. Mushrooms are sauteed. Onions are softened slowly in oil. Four pans, four methods, one pizza — which is why it takes the kitchen longer than a Diavola.

Courgettes are sliced lengthways and grilled dry on a ribbed pan. Peppers are roasted whole, sweated in a covered bowl and peeled. Mushrooms go into a wide pan in two batches, and the salt waits until they have browned. Onions are softened slowly in oil until they are sweet, which takes twenty minutes and cannot be hurried at a higher heat.

## How we bake it

By the time this pizza reaches the oven the work is over: eighty-five seconds at 429 °C, finishing a base and a cheese while everything above them is already cooked. That is the single reason it is not watery.

Everything on top is already cooked, so the oven is only finishing the base and the cheese. That is the whole reason the pizza is not watery.

## In numbers

- dough: 264 g
- courgette: 40 g, grilled dry on a ribbed pan
- pepper: 42 g, roasted whole, sweated under a cover, then peeled
- mushroom 40 g sauteed · onion 30 g softened twenty minutes in oil
- tomato: 83 g · fior di latte: 92 g
- the oven: 429 °C for eighty-five seconds, which is the shortest part of making it
- four pans and four methods: the longest preparation in the house

## What guests ask

**“Is the Vegetariana vegan?”**

No — it has mozzarella. The vegan one is the Verdure, and it is not this pizza with the cheese scraped off; it is built differently.

**“Why are the peppers peeled?”**

Because pepper skin does not soften in the oven and comes away in the mouth. Roasting and peeling is twenty minutes of work that nobody notices, which is the point.

**“Can I have it without the mushrooms?”**

Yes. Then it is close to the Ortolana, which is aubergine instead of mushroom and worth trying as it is.

## How it goes wrong

- raw vegetables on the dough: water in the cheese and a base that never crisps
- unpeeled roasted peppers: the skin lifts off in the mouth
- onions softened fast at high heat: bitter instead of sweet
- all four vegetables in one pan: nothing browns, everything steams

## House note

It is vegetarian, not vegan: the mozzarella stays. Guests who want the vegan version are sent to the Verdure, and the bot knows the difference.

Elena's phrase for the sad pizza has entered the kitchen's vocabulary. A cook who is told their pizza looks sad knows exactly what is meant.
